Technical Papers
Today’s research shapes tomorrow’s possibilities.
A cornerstone of SIGGRAPH, the Technical Papers program presents groundbreaking scholarly research advancing computer graphics and interactive techniques. From foundational discoveries to emerging approaches and real-world applications, Technical Papers explores ideas that deepen our understanding, challenge established thinking, and open new directions across disciplines.
Share original research with a global community of experts — or discover the breakthroughs redefining how we create, visualize, interact with, and understand the world around us.
